About this conversion

MOTOTRBO represents Radio IDs and Talkgroup IDs as the lower three octets of a network IP address. The first octet is the configured CAI or Group CAI value.

Radio IDs use the range 1–16,776,415 on general MOTOTRBO systems. Capacity Plus Radio IDs are limited to 1–65,535.

Default values are CAI 12 for individual Radio IDs and Group CAI 225 for Talkgroup IDs. Systems may be configured differently.

For authoritative configuration details, reserved values, and system-specific limitations, consult official Motorola MOTOTRBO documentation.
